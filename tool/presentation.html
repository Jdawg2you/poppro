Three sentences, then a question. Clients don't buy insurance, they buy confidence.

“That sounds expensive.”

I understand how you feel — most people do when they're comparing it to the $0 plans in the ads. What they found is these save money over a year because they pay from the first dollar. There's no $8,000 deductible to get through first. Can I show you how it pays you back on the visits you actually make?

“I already have something through work or my spouse.”

Glad you're already covered. Clients felt the same, and found the work plan left gaps — dental, accident, income protection — and a plan that pays them directly filled them. Open to seeing how this backs up what you've got?

“I want to think about it.”

I understand, it's a real decision. What clients found is that having it in writing made it easier, and that approval is based on your health today, not in a month. Let's reserve your approval while you think it over. No harm done if you pass. Sound fair?

“I need to talk to my spouse.”

Smart — it's a household decision. Clients found it worked better to get the spouse on a short call so you both hear it from me. Tomorrow evening, or Saturday morning for ten minutes?

“I can't afford that right now.”

Budgeting is real and I'd rather you say it. Clients felt that way until they priced one ER visit — three to five thousand dollars. If I build something that fits your budget and still protects you, worth looking at together?

“I want something with no deductible.”

Most people do. These aren't high-deductible plans — they're defined-benefit, paying a set amount from day one. Can I walk you through a real example?

“Why not just stick with the ACA plan?”

People feel that way, especially with a subsidy. What they found is narrow networks, high deductibles and a rate rise every year. This renews at the same price, on the First Health PPO. Want to see it side by side?

“I've never heard of ManhattanLife.”

Fair — not a household name. Around since 1850, one of the oldest insurers in the country. Clients found the private PPO through First Health gave them more control and fewer restrictions. Want to see their AM Best rating and the network?

“I'm healthy, I don't need insurance.”

That's exactly why now is the time. People felt that way until an accident or a surprise bill. Being healthy is what locks in this rate and holds it to 65. Makes sense to protect your good health while it's cheapest?

“Can you just send me some information?”

Absolutely. What clients found is that reading it alone didn't show them what they personally qualify for. I'll send it and walk you through it in five minutes — this evening or tomorrow morning?
