What it fills in, and what to check

✓ Fills in, from what was said

  • Names, state, ZIP, dates of birth and ages
  • Address, phone and email, if they came up
  • Who is on the quote, and the kids
  • Income, medications and conditions they told you
  • What they pay today, if they said
  • A brief at the top of the intake with every open item

✗ Leaves blank on purpose

  • Anything nobody asked. Blank means “not asked”, never “no”
  • Tobacco, if nobody asked. MyPopPro can only store yes or no, so an unasked tobacco question shows as “no”. The brief flags it as NEVER ASKED: ask before you quote
  • A medication it can’t spell. It flags it in the brief instead
  • Anything it would have to guess

Before you quote from it: read the brief at the top of the intake, confirm every medication and condition with the client, and check the state. A number the AI worked out itself is marked MODELLED. Never read a modelled number to a client as a quote.

🔴 Your details never go in the file

A client file holds the client, never the agent: no agency, phone, email, NPN, bio, booking link or Direction Rx code. MyPopPro takes them out when it saves and fills in yours when you open a file. That way, a file built by anyone opens as yours.

For that to work, save your own Setup once: MyPopPro → Setup → Save my Setup.

Keeping it current

When MyPopPro adds a field, the prompt on this page gets updated. Your copy has its version stamped at the top. The first time you build a file in a conversation, the AI checks this page and tells you in one line if your copy is older. If it is, come back and copy the prompt again.

An older prompt still works. Its files load fine, but they may miss the newest fields. There’s nothing to install: copying the prompt again is the update.

Keeping it current

Be mindful of personal information

A client file is personal information: names, dates of birth, address, phone, health details. It’s fine to keep it on your own computer and in MyPopPro. That’s what it’s for.

  • Don’t email it, post it, or send it to anyone. If another agent needs the client, talk to them about it first.
  • Use your own ClaudeChatGPT account, and delete the conversation when you no longer need it.
  • If a Social Security or driver’s licence number comes up on the call, it goes in the client’s notes. That’s fine, because it stays on your computer. The printed client profile shows only the last 4 digits. A file that holds one still needs extra care: never email, text or post it.
