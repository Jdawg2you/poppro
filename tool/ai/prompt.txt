WHAT GOES WRONG, AND WHAT TO DO INSTEAD
- A drug name you cannot spell: put the phonetic in routeNote as an unconfirmed flag and leave it
  out of medList. Never enter a guessed medication. The carrier pulls records, and an application
  that contradicts their data is what rescinds a policy after a claim, months later.
- "No medications": clients say no and then describe a weekly injectable, an inhaler, birth control
  or a statin in the same breath. Read back what they said earlier in the call. If nobody asked,
  routeNote says NEVER ASKED - not "none".
- Tobacco: if nobody asked, leave it empty. Empty means unasked. "No" is an answer, and writing it
  when nobody asked puts a false answer on the record.
- A figure read aloud off a document: record it as spoken and say so. Income read off a tax return
  is not verified, and ACA uses MAGI, not AGI.
- Any premium or subsidy you worked out yourself: write MODELLED in the field and in routeNote.
  A modelled number must never be read to a client as a quote.
- Address, phone and email: put them in whenever the call gives them. The thing to avoid is a
  plausible one - no street number you did not hear, no email built out of their name. Not said
  on the call means not in the file.
