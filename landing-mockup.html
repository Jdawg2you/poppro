How it works

Manage every client from hello to a Package of Protection — in place and reviewed.

Six steps, and MyPopPro walks you through each one. Every step fills the next, so you never type the same thing twice.

Discovery call

The script, built in. Type answers into the blanks as the client talks — household, health, medications, doctors, budget, their “why”.

Intake

Already filled from the call. Check it, add anything missing, confirm their doctors are in network.

Quote

Good / Better / Best, priced from the carriers’ own rate tables for their state and ages — with their true cost against what they pay now.

Present

A presentation script, screen by screen, with their name, numbers, doctors and medications filled in. Enroll as presented — or adjust the plan right there.

Enroll

The applications in call order, each person’s details ready to copy, and the thank-you email and text written for you.

Benefits Review

After approval: confirm the plan, walk them through the portal, telehealth and claims app, and send their client care card.
